## Getting Started & Agency Projects

When I joined **3spin** in 2018, the company was still largely structured like an agency. At first, I worked on short-term projects, such as web applications and apps for trade fairs.

Many of these projects involved playing **360° videos on VR headsets or tablets** with interactive elements in the video.

From the beginning, I was able to apply my experience with **Unity3D** and develop a cross-platform **media player**, which was subsequently used in several projects.

---

## HoloConference – Lead Developer

After a few months at 3spin, I was given my first project as **Lead Developer**.

**HoloConference** was an exploratory research project for a client and was intended to serve as a prototype for conference software developed exclusively for the **Microsoft HoloLens**.

### Concept

In a HoloConference, users could meet as holograms, communicate with one another, and interact with objects in the room.

Together with my team, we designed and implemented various tools:

- Digital whiteboards
- 3D pens
- Virtual Post-it notes
- Interaction with objects in the room

### Technical Challenges

One of the biggest challenges was transmitting a **3D scan of the users within an acceptable amount of time** while keeping the positions of all participants synchronized.

Over a period of approximately one year, we developed a functional **online prototype**, which was successfully presented at the **DLD Conference in Munich**.

Due to complications during the COVID-19 pandemic, further development of HoloConference was subsequently suspended indefinitely.

## 3spin DREAM – VR/AR Training Platform

After development of HoloConference was halted, I joined the development team for **3spin DREAM**.

3spin DREAM is a platform that allows users to **create and manage VR training programs**. The software runs on common **VR and AR headsets**.

### Development

The development team worked with:

- **Unity3D**
- **React**
- **Microsoft Mixed Reality Toolkit**

I worked both as a **software developer and UX designer**.

A particular challenge was that 3spin DREAM is used not only to run training programs, but also as an **editor for creating those trainings**.

As a result, all functions had to work reliably and intuitively:

- In **VR environments**
- In **AR environments**
- Both while using and while creating training programs

### UX Design & Prototyping

As a UX designer, I developed various features and tools to make creating training programs **more intuitive and efficient**.

I initially implemented many of these concepts as **interactive prototypes**.

These prototypes were then tested with users to validate their suitability for official implementation.

At the same time, I contributed to the development of official **3spin DREAM releases** and often took responsibility for the **final technical implementation** of the features I had designed.
