To me, UX design means making complex systems not only intuitive, but also enjoyable to use. It requires more than a good eye for visual quality; it also requires a deep understanding of how a system is structured. As a software developer, I can dive into a system’s core functionality to identify bottlenecks, user obstacles, and challenges for developers. A significant part of my work involves moderating complex discussions between designers, developers, and managers. Understanding the goals and challenges of both worlds can significantly accelerate problem-solving throughout every phase of a project.
